Driver Monitor · on-device

Fatigue and distraction monitoring, running on your phone

Mount the phone so the front camera can see your face, then start. The camera feed is processed on this device and never uploaded — there is no server to send it to.

  • Microsleep — eyes closed past 1.5 s
  • Drowsiness — PERCLOS over a rolling minute
  • Yawning — counted when the mouth closes again
  • Distraction — head off the road past 2 s
  • Safety index — each lapse weighed by how bad, how trustworthy the measurement is, and the time of day

The first few seconds calibrate: look straight ahead while it learns your neutral head position, and keep blinking normally while it learns your eye shape. A fixed blink threshold misreads some people's eyes as permanently shut.

The hour selector overrides the clock so the circadian weighting can be shown without waiting for 3 am: the same lapse costs more at night, when a fatigue detection is more likely to be real. Fatigue penalties are multiplied; attention penalties are not.

Ported from the desktop system in this repository. Head pose comes from MediaPipe's own facial transformation matrix rather than solvePnP, which avoids the mirror ambiguity that made a turned head read as turned the other way. Hands on the wheel, seatbelt and phone detection are not in this build.
